Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect the following information:
• Username and game account data
• Email address (used only for account creation and sign-in)
• Game progress (levels, gold, countries owned)
• Device information (device type, OS version)
• App usage analytics and session data
• Push notification preferences (if enabled)

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:
• Operate and improve the game
• Track your game progress and leaderboard rankings
• Provide game analytics and understand user behavior
• Send optional game updates and notifications
• Maintain the integrity of multiplayer features

3. Sign-In Options

GeoConquest offers Sign in with Apple, Sign in with Google, and email sign-in. These services share only your name and email address with us. If you use Sign in with Apple, you may choose to hide your email address, and we will receive only a private relay address.

4. Advertising

GeoConquest displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may use a device advertising identifier to serve and measure ads. On supported devices you can control or reset this identifier in your device settings, and you will be asked for tracking permission where required. You can learn more in Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. In-App Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed securely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card details.

6. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal data. Your username appears on public leaderboards. Analytics and advertising providers may receive anonymized or device-level data to help us operate and improve the game and serve ads.

7. Data Retention

• Account data: Retained while you play
• Analytics: Anonymized after 90 days
• Leaderboard data: Retained for competitive integrity

8. Your Rights

• Delete your account anytime
• Opt out of analytics
• Turn off push notifications
• Request your data

9. Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. All communications are encrypted. We regularly audit our security practices.

10. Children's Privacy

GeoConquest is appropriate for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.
